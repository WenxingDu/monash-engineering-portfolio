# monash-engineering-portfolio
Technical and academic projects completed during my studies at Monash University.

Each subdirectory contains a dedicated README describing the scope, methodology, and focus of the corresponding projects.

Following the completion of the final year project, I am continuing to work on related research with my FYP supervisor and a former project teammate. The goal is to develop the work into a conference paper submission.


## Project Areas

### Power System Projects
Research-oriented work focused on low-inertia power systems and grid-forming converters, including my Final Year Project on synthetic inertia from BESS.

### Artificial Intelligence Projects
Applied machine learning and deep learning projects, with a focus on model behaviour, training dynamics, and limitations rather than benchmark optimisation.

### Control Systems Projects
Classical and discrete-time control system design projects, emphasising modelling, stability analysis, and control-oriented reasoning.

### Electronics Design
Analogue circuit design projects involving component-level analysis, trade-offs, and simulation-driven validation.

### Embedded Systems Projects
FPGA-based and embedded system designs involving hardware–software co-design, real-time constraints, and concurrency.

### Signal Processing Projects
Projects applying frequency-domain and time–frequency analysis to real signals, with an emphasis on filtering decisions guided by spectral reasoning.
